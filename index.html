A Caesar cipher slides every letter a fixed number of places along the alphabet. To undo it, slide each letter the other way by the same amount.     Cipher typeMorse code  ·  Vigenère cipher

    Cartographer's notes

    Morse code writes letters as short and long signals. A Vigenère cipher is a Caesar cipher whose slide changes with every letter, following a repeating key word; to undo it, slide back by each letter of the key in turn. A cipher is only as locked as its key is hidden, and keys are often kept somewhere other than the lock. Use the buttons to slow or pause the lamp. Type a single word below.

      III

      The Shoals

      Four walls to a pen, and a sailor's secret in every one.

      Cipher typePigpen cipher  ·  Grid path

      Cartographer's notes

      The pigpen cipher swaps each letter for the outline of the box it sits in, taken from grids and crosses, with a dot marking the later half of the alphabet. Once the symbols speak, treat the words as orders. Using the grid: begin on the square marked with the skull. Each order gives a direction and a number of squares; the top of the grid is north. Move exactly that many squares, with each leg starting where the last one ended. Jot down the letter you stand on at every stop, remembering the square you began on, and read them in the order you visited them. Clicking a square marks it, which helps you keep your place. Type a single word below.

        IV

        The Sexton's Psalm

        The sexton buried his psalm in the dark, and gave it two voices.

        Cipher typeBacon's cipher

        Cartographer's notes

        Bacon's cipher hides letters inside ordinary writing. Instead of changing the letters, it changes their style: here, an upright letter stands for A and a slanted (italic) letter, which is also gold, stands for B. Every hidden letter takes five of them. How to read it: move the lantern over the psalm, and write down A or B for each letter in turn, ignoring spaces. Cut your string into groups of five, then look each group up in the table below the crypt. Type the word you uncover.

        The crypt beneath Sexton's Reach has no windows. Carry the lantern; move it with your pointer, or tap and drag. The psalm was copied, so the old log says, from a book of the New Atlantis.
